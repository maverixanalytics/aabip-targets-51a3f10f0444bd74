Capturing leads

Lead capture now runs through this site instead of texting photos around. On any physician card, tap 📷 Badge photo + note.

  1. Take the badge photo — name, institution and QR code should be readable.
  2. Add a note: product interest, interest level (wants a demo / wants to trial / already using), clinical focus, how you met, suggested next step.
  3. Pick your name from the dropdown. It remembers you after the first time.
  4. Hit Save. If you have no signal it queues on your phone and uploads itself the moment you're back on.
For anyone not on the target list, use the purple Off-list lead button — bottom-left on your phone, top of the page on a laptop. No need to type their name; the badge photo carries it.

Install it before you fly

Open this site once on hotel wifi Wednesday night and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. That installs it as an app and pre-caches all 44 physician photos.

Convention centre wifi is famously bad. Installed and pre-cached, the whole list — photos, talk tracks, checkboxes — keeps working with no signal at all, and anything you tap syncs once you're back in range.
